## Description (max 4000)
```
Vektor is a calculator you can type to in plain English. Write "10 mi in km", "1,800 THB in EUR", or "15% of 240" and the answer appears as you type — no buttons, no modes. It lives in your menu bar, one keystroke away, and floats over whatever you're working on, even full-screen apps.

It began as a fast notepad calculator and grew a handful of tools for the things people actually open a calculator to do.

WHAT IT DOES

Calculator
• Plain-English math, line by line, with live results
• Unit conversions — length, weight, speed, area, data and more
• Live currency and crypto — "0.5 BTC in USD", "1,500 USD to EUR"
• Time zones — "1430 Berlin in Hong Kong"
• Percentages, running totals, variables and inline notes

Finance
• Loan and mortgage payments
• Real-estate deal analysis
• Tip and split, inflation, retirement, savings goals, travel budgets

Aviation (for flight training and planning reference)
• METAR / TAF / ATIS decoding
• E6B flight computer, weight & balance, density altitude, wind components
• Interactive airport map with live METAR colouring (VFR / MVFR / IFR / LIFR)

Stocks
• Score a public company against a durable-competitive-advantage framework
• Watchlist with at-a-glance metrics

Built for macOS
• A menu-bar app you can summon instantly, even over a full-screen Space
• Keyboard-first, with an always-on-top option and launch at login
• Your math stays on your Mac. The internet is used only to fetch live rates, quotes and weather. No account, no tracking.

TRY IT FREE
Use everything free for 7 days. Then unlock Vektor forever with a single purchase — no subscription.
```

Vektor Calculator ("Vektor") is a macOS app by Patrick Grauel. It is built to keep your data on your device.

None. Vektor has no account system, no analytics, and no advertising. We do not collect, store, or share personal information.

On-device data
Your calculations, notes, settings, and any API keys you enter are stored locally on your Mac and are never sent to us.
